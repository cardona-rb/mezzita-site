# PedidosYa (Delivery Hero) in Bolivia: restaurant terms and sales strategy

Research date: 2026-10-23. Method: WebSearch/WebFetch. Official pages (socios.pedidosya.com.bo, centrodesocios.pedidosya.com/bo/*, partner help) returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch, so Bolivia official claims below come from SEARCH SNIPPETS of those pages (marked [snippet]). Nothing here was read in full from PedidosYa Bolivia pages.

## 1. Requirements and fees

### Verified for Bolivia (via search snippets of official pages, fetched 2026-10-23)
- Registration: online form at https://socios.pedidosya.com.bo/es ; needs identity document OR NIT, and a bank account (business or owner's name) [snippet].
- Menu: partner blog says active bank account + digital menu loaded in self-service portal with names, prices, photos, availability [snippet] https://centrodesocios.pedidosya.com/bo/blog/todo-lo-que-necesitas-saber-para-vender-en-pedidosya-en-bolivia/
- Commission model: percentage per order; "can vary by country, business location and type of partner"; funds delivery logistics, app security, marketing [snippet] https://socios.pedidosya.com.bo/es
- New-partner promo: 10% commission for 30 days plus free PedidosYa Plus (60 days on one page; another partner page says 30-day Plus free trial; they conflict) [snippet] https://socios.pedidosya.com.bo/es and https://centrodesocios.pedidosya.com/blog/beneficios-exclusivos-para-socios-pedidosya-haz-que-tu-negocio-crezca-desde-el-dia-uno/
- Payout: weekly settlement, bank transfer; portal shows commissions, sales, payment dates [snippet] socios.pedidosya.com.bo; centrodesocios.pedidosya.com/bo
- Own riders allowed; commission differs for own fleet vs PedidosYa logistics [snippet] https://centrodesocios.pedidosya.com/bo/blog/como-funciona-pedidosya-para-restaurantes-y-negocios-de-comida/
- Growth products named in a Bolivia partner blog title: "Destacados, Creditos y Plus" (featured placement, credit/financing, subscription) https://centrodesocios.pedidosya.com/bo/blog/tres-formas-en-que-pedidosya-impulsa-el-crecimiento-de-tu-restaurante-socios-creditos-y-plus/ (content not retrieved; prices unknown).
- Coverage: Santa Cruz, La Paz, Cochabamba, El Alto, Sucre, Tarija [snippet]; a 2020s-era note cites 12 cities and 5,000+ allied merchants (dplnews snippet, undated, UNVERIFIED).

### NOT found for Bolivia (do not assume)
Standard post-promo commission %, onboarding fee, tablet/device fee, minimum ratings, response-time rules, exclusivity, packaging rules, cancellation penalties, ad prices. None published in anything retrievable.
- chejefe.com (third-party, pub 2026-06-08, upd 2026-10-07) says PedidosYa publishes no standard post-signup rate in any country; effective rate set per contract; Bolivia not covered. https://www.chejefe.com/blog/independencia-de-las-apps/comisiones-pedidosya-rentabilidad

### Other countries (reference only)
- Chile: 20% promo first 60 days; Peru: 10% promo + Plus free 30 days; Peru unofficial 2026 comparison (Panca) 22-28% with delivery; Argentina 10% promo + Plus 30 days (chejefe, above).
- Argentina 2021 pandemic-era caps: 10%+IVA marketplace only, 18%+IVA with logistics; expired 2021-09-30 (chejefe). Santa Fe cap 25%+IVA (2020) https://www.santafe.gov.ar/noticias/noticia/268464/ ; BAE Negocios 2020/2021 reports earlier commissions above 30%.
- Third-party (carroscomo.com, unverified, not Bolivia): ~13% without logistics, up to ~21% with.
- Paraguay onboarding: RUC, business name, bank account, product info (dplnews snippet, undated).
- Argentina: orders/disputes: restaurants cover 85% of a disputed product cost (Letra P, 2026-04-14, owner claim).

### Owner's 25% estimate (20-35%)
Consistent with: Argentina historical ">30%", Rosario owners claiming effective ~35% weekly deductions all-in (below), Peru 22-28%. No Bolivia source confirms. Treat as plausible, unverified.

## 2. Sales strategy toward restaurants
- Self-serve form funnel at socios.pedidosya.com.bo ("Registra tu negocio en Bolivia"); partner blog (Centro de Socios) as content marketing; portal de autogestion for menu upload [snippets].
- Hooks: low promo commission (10% x 30 days) + free Plus trial; weekly payouts; choice of own or PedidosYa riders; visibility (Destacados), credit, Plus.
- Ordatic (third-party integrator, undated) paraphrases PedidosYa pitch: heavy marketing spend gives visibility, "no fixed costs".
- Fintech/credit to restaurants: CEO says credit is offered for working capital/expansion (Peru Retail snippet) https://www.peru-retail.com/pedidosya-evoluciona-incursiona-en-fintech-y-expande-servicios-mas-alla-del-delivery/
- Bolivia strategy: managing director Diego Sulzer (ex commercial manager) said focus on local stores and dark kitchens, PedidosYa Market digital supermarket; entered Bolivia 2018 via Netcomidas (article 2022-06-21) https://dplnews.com/bolivia-diego-sulzer-nuestro-norte-es-abrir-el-negocio-en-tiendas-locales-y-dark-kitchens/
- PedidosYa Plus (consumer subscription: free shipping + discounts). Restaurant participation is voluntary and benefits are co-financed with merchants (Uruguay launch note, montevideo.com.uy, undated; NOT Bolivia). Chile review (chocale.cl 2025-09): PedidosYa gives no service-fee discount; only 58% of merchants in test offered Plus benefits. Bank-card tie-ins are bank-funded. Bolivia specifics unverified.

## 4. Restaurant complaints

Owner/chamber claims, no PedidosYa reply. https://www.letrap.com.ar/economia/rosario-pedidosya-se-queda-el-35-la-recaudacion-y-desata-una-guerra-los-gastronomicos-n5423159
- San Juan, Argentina (Diario de Cuyo, 2020-06-26): 27% per sale, 30% if customer pays by card. https://www.diariodecuyo.com.ar/noticias/hay-quejas-de-los-comerciantes-por-las-altas-comisiones-que-les-cobra-pedidos-ya-225169.html
- Chile (reclamos.cl, 2024-05): single owner says contracted 30% charged as 31-35%. Anecdote.
- Unsourced generic sites (numeroservicioalcliente.com) mention payout delays and commissions above contract; low reliability.
- Bolivia: NO restaurant-owner complaints found in press/forums. Bolivian consumer complaints only (tuquejasuma.com/pedidos-ya-bolivia). Data ownership: nothing found.
