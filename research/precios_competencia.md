## 3) Observations on how the Bolivian market prices

1. Websites are sold one-time, bundled with year-1 domain and hosting "free" (TecnoWeb, gcom, KOM). The recurring revenue is the renewal after year 1, which nobody states a price for. A site priced monthly would be the outlier.
2. Social media and ads are the only recurring services, always monthly, usually with a minimum contract (CMB 3 months on the sub-page, search snippet of Emprendimientos 6 months, DeChaLi discount for 6+ months) and the first month paid in advance (Logicalweb FAQ). Logicalweb justifies this with "resultados a medio y largo plazo".
3. Currency is mixed. Providers of the Cochabamba cluster (RedPlan, Logicalweb) show a bare "$" for monthly retainers, while most La Paz/other providers (CMB, Expertosis, DeChaLi, Chicha, TecnoWeb, Novara) price in Bs and stress it ("Sin cobros en dolares", "Precios en bolivianos"). Several note taxes: Expertosis "incluye IVA", DeChaLi "antes de impuestos", KOM "no incluyen impuestos". Compare tax treatment before comparing prices.
4. Ad spend is always separate and is itself a visible number: Bs 300-600 (RedPlan), Bs 990-2,800 suggested (DeChaLi), Bs 150-400 bundled by Logicalweb. The management fee is a flat monthly plan, not a percentage of spend, in every verified case.
5. Discounting is by prepayment/term, not by haggling on the list: CMB annual -10%, Chicha annual -20% and 14-day free trial, DeChaLi 6+ month plans, Xmart annual -10% (unverified). Prices are "desde" (from) and "sujetos a cambio", with detail "on request" (Expertosis). Premium agencies (Novara, CMB Enterprise) sit 3-5x above the entry tier and list unverifiable social proof, so treat their tiers as anchors, not market medians. Local-SEO/GBP, review plates and bundles are essentially unpriced in Bolivia: an open gap.

## Exchange-rate caveat

The instructed 6.96 is the legacy official peg. Sources found today show it is no longer a realistic conversion: eju.tv (25 Jan 2026, https://eju.tv/2026/01/el-dolar-paralelo-se-acerca-al-valor-referencial-del-bcb-que-implica-para-el-pais/): parallel "Bs 9,54 compra / Bs 9,53 venta", BCB reference "Bs 9,16 compra / Bs 9,35 venta", official "estable desde 2011 en alrededor de Bs 6,96". Infobae 6 Aug 2026 (https://www.infobae.com/noticias/2026/08/06/precio-del-dolar-hoy-en-bolivia-cotizacion-de-apertura-del-6-de-agosto/): USD at "11,81 bolivianos" (Dow Jones) while "el tipo de cambio oficial permanece en Bs 6,96, su uso es cada vez menos relevante". Search snippets (not opened, UNVERIFIED) cite a CEED/CADECOCRUZ note of ~Bs 11.50 (13 Aug 2026) and a BCB weekend rate of Bs 12,13 (1-3 Aug 2026). No October 2026 figure found. Consequence: every USD-priced row above is worth materially more Bs than the 6.96 column shows, and Bs-priced rows may be stale.
